# Composition brief — PenguinLaw launch video

## Hook
"Five real clips — Powell, Obama, a Fed presser, Gen Z investors — read
end-to-end. Transcript, fused emotion timeline, and analyst observations in
one screen."

## Story arc (20s)
1. **Identity** — name + what it is (voice & bias analysis console).
2. **Proof** — real clips exist and are read LIVE.
3. **The magic** — the per-3s emotion timeline (fusion of voice + wording),
   with the honest guard (low confidence → neutral).
4. **The payoff** — a traceable analyst read that ties an observation to a
   timestamp, a transcript line, and voice probabilities.
5. **Call to action** — "You built it. Now brag."

## Visual language
- Dark slate background (#0D111B), white title, red accent (#E74C3C).
- Emotion color coding: angry=red, sad=blue, neutral=grey, disgust=green,
  fear=purple, happy=yellow.
- Timeline sweeps left→right; cards rise in; scenes fade.

## Callouts / copy
- Title: **PenguinLaw** — *Voice & Bias Analysis Console*
- Tagline: *Every word. Every tone. Read in one place.*
- Timeline caption: *each 3s window tagged from acoustic + lexical fusion*
- Guard note: *low-confidence windows fall back to neutral (guard)*
- Analyst panel: type=hedging · when=0:20 · evidence=“This is more
  complicated.” · voice=angry 0.45 / happy 0.27 · why=nuance to press on

## Honesty guardrails
- No claim of verified per-clip accuracy (see README/JOURNEY).
- Observations are labeled as model output, not ground truth.
